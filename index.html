Advancing Accessible Digital Learning in South Africa

ChatGPT Foundation is a South African non-profit company established in 2024. We are developing a foundation for future work in accessible artificial intelligence education, e-commerce and digital operations.

Our long-term purpose is to help people from different educational backgrounds build practical knowledge, confidence and readiness for a changing digital economy.

About the Foundation Explore Our Focus

Why We Exist

Rapid technological change creates new opportunities, but it can also widen existing social and economic inequalities. ChatGPT Foundation seeks to make practical digital knowledge more accessible, particularly for unemployed and underserved individuals in South Africa.

Our Focus

Accessible AI Learning

Developing future learning opportunities that introduce artificial intelligence in a practical, understandable and inclusive way.

E-commerce and Digital Operations

Exploring training in online business management, customer service and digital operations that may support employment readiness.

Business and Community Support

Building a foundation for future collaboration with businesses and community initiatives interested in practical and responsible uses of digital technology.

Current Status

Establishing our public presence

ChatGPT Foundation is currently establishing its public presence and developing its future programme framework. No public courses or applications are currently open.
