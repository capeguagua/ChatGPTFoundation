About

Who We Are

ChatGPT Foundation is an independent South African non-profit company registered in July 2024.

The organisation was established to explore how accessible education in artificial intelligence, e-commerce and online operations may contribute to employment readiness, business development and inclusive participation in the digital economy.

Our Mission

To expand access to practical digital knowledge and create pathways through which individuals and communities can engage confidently with emerging technologies.

Our Vision

A society in which people from different educational and academic backgrounds have meaningful opportunities to learn about and participate in the digital economy.

Our Values

Accessibility Practical learning Inclusion Responsible use of technology Transparency and accountability Community development
