Our Focus

Future Areas of Work

The Foundation is developing its institutional framework around the following areas:

01

AI Education

Accessible introductory learning that connects artificial intelligence concepts with practical everyday and workplace applications.

02

E-commerce Skills

Future learning opportunities related to online shops, digital customer service and online business operations.

03

Employment Readiness

Exploring ways to help participants understand emerging forms of digital work and develop relevant practical skills.

04

Business Support

Future knowledge-sharing initiatives for South African organisations interested in adopting artificial intelligence and digital tools.

05

Community Engagement

Potential partnerships and community initiatives that promote inclusive and socially beneficial uses of technology.

These areas describe the Foundation’s intended direction. Specific programmes will be announced only after they have been formally approved and prepared.
